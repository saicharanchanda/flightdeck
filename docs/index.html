Architecture

EKS 1.36 on 2 × m7i-flex.large in us-east-1 — public subnets, no NAT gateway; images from GHCR, tagged by git SHA. One ALB in front of the gateway and Grafana; Prometheus scrapes, Alloy ships logs to Loki. Two IAM identity mechanisms, used on purpose so they can be compared.

Incidents

Game days under steady in-cluster load (k6, 20 req/s). Charts are rendered from the metrics Prometheus recorded during each run. Each writeup includes a timeline, every query used, and what didn't go as expected.

Game day 02 · headline

Latency up 320× — and the autoscaler wanted to scale in

800ms injected into every gateway request for 24 minutes. The HPA's CPU signal moved from 12% to 15% of a 70% target; replicas stayed at 2.

Its status read TooFewReplicas: the desired replica count is less than the minimum. Waiting isn't work, so CPU-based autoscaling sees a slow service as an idle one. A forced extra pod then got 0% of in-cluster traffic: a Service balances connections, not requests.

Game day 01

Error spike: paged in 9.5 min — would be ~29 in production

30% of gateway requests returned 500. Every error carried route="unmatched" and finished in 25µs: rejected before routing.

The 1h window held only 6 minutes of clean traffic. With a full clean hour, a 30% error rate needs ~29 minutes to cross 14.4×. The ticket fired 5 minutes after the fix.

Game day 04

Postgres at zero: an honest probe, and clients still hung for 5s

flights' readiness probe really checks the database — so every replica went NotReady at once and the Service emptied. 100% errors for 12 minutes.

Almost every failure was a 5-second hang (504), not a fast error: flights had no DB timeout of its own. No alert named Postgres. Recovery: 30.5s, no restarts.

Full writeup →
Game day 03

OOMKill: Go ignores the cgroup limit — GOMEMLIMIT took restarts from 12 to 0

go_gc_gomemlimit_bytes read MaxInt64. At 32Mi that didn't matter — flights' ~8Mi is flat even at 7.5× traffic. At 6Mi it did.

At 6Mi it crash-looped: 12 restarts in 10 minutes. Same limit with GOMEMLIMIT: 0. The "floor" was heap the unaware GC chose to keep. The chart now sets it to 90% of every limit.
